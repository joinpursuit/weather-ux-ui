# Weather UX-UI Application - 90 Second Presentation Script

## Opening (10 seconds)

"Hi everyone! I'm excited to show you our Weather UX-UI application. This is a modern, responsive weather app that delivers real-time weather data with an intuitive and visually engaging interface."

---

## Key Features & Design (40 seconds)

**"Let me walk you through what makes this app special:**

1. **Real-Time Location-Based Weather** - The app uses your device's geolocation to automatically detect your current location and fetch live weather data from the Open-Meteo API. You can instantly see your city name displayed at the top.

2. **Current Weather Display** - The app shows you the current temperature, weather condition, and high/low temperatures for the day. The background dynamically changes based on weather conditions—sunny days have a bright background, rain shows darker clouds, and storms display dramatic effects.

3. **Hourly & 9-Day Forecasts** - Users can view hourly weather updates and a detailed 9-day forecast with temperature ranges and precipitation probabilities."

---

## UX Highlight: Temperature Unit Toggle (25 seconds)

**"Now, here's a great UX feature I want to highlight—the temperature unit toggle button.**

_[Point to/click the toggle]_

**See these two buttons at the top right? 'C' for Celsius and 'F' for Fahrenheit. This is a seamless, user-friendly interaction.**

- Click the toggle, and instantly all temperatures across the entire app—current, hourly, and 9-day forecast—convert and update in real-time.
- The active button is visually highlighted so users always know which unit they're viewing.
- No page reload, no confusion—just smooth, responsive design.

This demonstrates excellent UX principles: **clarity, responsiveness, and user control.**"

---

## Closing (15 seconds)

"The app combines clean visual design with practical functionality. The dynamic backgrounds, interactive icons, and intuitive controls create an engaging experience. Whether you need a quick weather check or detailed forecasts, this app delivers both style and substance. Thank you!"

---

## Total Time: ~90 seconds

**Tip:** Practice speaking naturally, and don't rush the temperature toggle demonstration—let them see it working!
